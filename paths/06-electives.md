# Electives

Stand-alone deep dives. Take them when a project or interest calls for them. Each lists its own prerequisites.

| Lesson | When to take it | Prereqs | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [EL-01 Time Series Forecasting](../lessons/electives/01-time-series-forecasting.md) | Your data has timestamps and you need to predict the future | CORE-04, CORE-05 | 10 h |
| [EL-02 Bayesian & Probabilistic ML](../lessons/electives/02-bayesian-probabilistic-ml.md) | You need uncertainty estimates, small-data inference, or A/B testing | CORE-03, MATH-03 | 10 h |
| [EL-03 Reinforcement Learning](../lessons/electives/03-reinforcement-learning.md) | You're curious about agents, games, robotics, or how RLHF works | DL-03 | 12 h |
| [EL-04 Recommender Systems](../lessons/electives/04-recommender-systems.md) | Feeds, shops, and "people also bought" | CORE-04, DL-02 | 9 h |
| [EL-05 Causal Inference & Uplift](../lessons/electives/05-causal-inference-uplift.md) | You need to know the effect of an action, not just a prediction | CORE-02, CORE-05, MATH-03 | 10 h |
| [EL-06 Anomaly & Outlier Detection](../lessons/electives/06-anomaly-detection.md) | Fraud, failures, intrusions, bad data | CORE-06, CORE-10 | 5 h |
| [EL-07 Mechanistic Interpretability](../lessons/electives/07-mechanistic-interpretability.md) | You want to know *how* transformers compute (AI safety research) | DL-06, CORE-08 | 10 h |
| [EL-08 GPU Programming for ML](../lessons/electives/08-gpu-programming.md) | You want to write fast kernels (CUDA concepts, Triton) | DL-07 | 10 h |
| [EL-09 Audio & Speech](../lessons/electives/09-audio-and-speech.md) | ASR, audio classification, voice | DL-04, DL-06 | 8 h |
| [EL-10 Deep Learning for Tabular Data](../lessons/electives/10-tabular-deep-learning.md) | You want an honest answer to "should I use a neural net on my table?" | CORE-05, CORE-07, DL-03 | 5 h |
